What you get

Built for kitchens that deliver or want direct online orders without marketplace commission.

Branded ordering page

Your logo, name, and colours on a clean link like /food/your-name. Customers order without downloading an app.

Live menu and photos

Categories, prices, and dish photos. Mark items unavailable from the staff dashboard so guests only see what is cooking.

Pickup and delivery

Customers choose pickup or delivery to areas you serve. You control fulfilment and your own riders — Leva does not take a delivery cut.

One login per restaurant. See new orders, update status, manage the menu, and toggle availability from any phone or laptop.

Order alerts

Telegram alert the moment an order lands so the kitchen can move fast. Order ID for the customer to track.

Payments your way

Paystack (card / transfer), bank receipt upload, pay on delivery, or a mix. Leva never takes a commission on food sales.
