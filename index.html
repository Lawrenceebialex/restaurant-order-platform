Questions owners ask.

Do you take a commission?

No. You pay ₦30,000 a month and keep every naira from your food sales.

Where do I set everything up?

Inside your private dashboard after you sign up. Your ordering page and QR menu go public only when you publish them.

What do my customers see?

An ordering link for cart and checkout, and a separate QR menu link for browsing prices at the table.

Can I make it look like my restaurant?

Yes. Choose your own colours and preview your branding in the dashboard before you go live.
